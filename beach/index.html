Two findings that change the plan

1. Do not spend miles on this trip. Portland to Honolulu is $379 round trip in cash, nonstop. Burning 56,400 miles plus $36 to avoid that fare is 0.61 cents per point, against your 2.0 bar. Cancun is worse at 0.32 and Cabo at 0.40. Every one of these is a cash flight. The 430k Chase points and 372k Amex points stay parked for the November Japan trip, where a business seat actually clears the bar.

2. You cannot harvest both expiring credits on one stay. The CSR $250 only fires through Chase's The Edit; the Amex $300 only fires through Amex Travel FHR or Hotel Collection. Different portals, one booking, one credit. Clean split: take the Amex $300 here in September, send the CSR $250 to November in Tokyo on your IHG status, and put the CSR $300 flexible travel credit against these flights, since that one is not portal-locked. That is $600 used across both trips instead of $550 fought over in one.

Before you scroll: everything here is priced on cash flights
