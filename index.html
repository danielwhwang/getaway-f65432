    How these numbers were built

    Lodging is mid-range, the kind of place that is clearly nice without being a splurge, and it does not double for a second person because you share the room. Food, local transport, and activities do scale with a second person. Every total carries a 10 percent buffer and is rounded to the nearest hundred. The points column assumes you cover the fare with Chase UR or Amex MR and pay only taxes and surcharges in cash.

    Exchange rates used, pulled 2026-08-18: 1 EUR = $1.158 · 100 MXN = $5.87 · 1000 JPY = $6.26. The yen at roughly 160 to the dollar is the single biggest swing factor on this page. Tokyo is priced like a bargain right now, and that is a currency story, not a Japan story.

    What is not included: your unused card credits. Depending on what is left for 2026, the Chase Edit credit, the select-hotel credit, the flexible travel credit, and the Amex second-half hotel credit could take several hundred dollars off any of these. Check both accounts before booking.
